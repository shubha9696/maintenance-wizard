# Blast Furnace SOP for Superheaters
To maintain the superheater in the Blast Furnace Area (BF-SH-001):
1. Clean the air intake filters every Tuesday at 9:00 AM.
2. The lubrication pressure must be kept between 4.2 bar and 4.8 bar.
3. If vibration levels exceed 5.5 mm/s, immediately trigger the safety bypass and contact the lead engineer, Dr. John Smith, at 555-0199.
4. Clean the nozzle with high-pressure nitrogen gas.
5. Do not use water under any circumstances to prevent thermal shock.
